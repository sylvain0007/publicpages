A personal assistant for everyday organisation

Turn information into useful next steps.

Empower Personal Assistant helps its user review email, organise tasks and contacts, and manage calendar information through a private assistant and Telegram.

What the assistant does

  • Reads connected Gmail message metadata and bodies, and attachments when used, to classify messages and prepare summaries.
  • Helps organise tasks and contacts in Notion.
  • Reads connected Google Calendar information and carries out approved calendar changes.
  • Presents proposed actions for review through Telegram; mail changes require user approval.

How connections are used

Google account access is granted through Google authorisation. Connected information may be processed by a configured external AI provider through OpenRouter and shared through Telegram for review. The assistant also keeps local records and backups.

This website explains the personal tool. It does not provide sign-in or access to the private assistant, and it does not collect Google credentials.
