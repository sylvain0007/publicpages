Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

Empower Personal Assistant is a personal tool for personal organisation. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the public issue tracker.

Information accessed and why

With account authorisation, the assistant accesses Gmail message metadata and bodies, and attachments when used, to classify email, prepare summaries and propose next steps. User-approved mail changes may be carried out. It reads Google Calendar information for scheduling and performs calendar changes with user approval. Tasks and contacts used by the assistant are stored in Notion.

Processing and sharing

Relevant content may be sent through OpenRouter to the configured external AI provider to fulfil assistant requests. Messages, summaries and proposed actions may be sent through Telegram for review. Notion stores task and contact records. The assistant also keeps local records, operational logs and backups. Information is therefore not confined to local storage.

Each connected service processes the information it receives under its own terms and privacy practices. The configured provider and service settings determine their handling of that information. Empower does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising or general-purpose model training.

Access and protection

The private assistant is operated for the owner's personal use. Account authorisation and configured service credentials control access to connected services. The public website does not expose the private assistant or its stored records.

Retention and deletion

Assistant records, logs and backups are retained until the owner deletes them. Revoking access in your Google account permissions stops future authorised access; it does not erase existing records. Deletion requires removing relevant records from local storage and backups, Notion, Telegram and external providers as applicable, subject to those services' retention practices. Contact the owner for help with deletion.

This public website

This static website has no forms, advertising or analytics added by Empower. GitHub hosts the site and may process technical request information, including IP addresses, under its own privacy practices.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
